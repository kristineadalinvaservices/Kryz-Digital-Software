# Kryz Digital Partners Brand Guidelines
Current version: v2, 20 November 2026 (black, neon pink and bright yellow). This replaces v1 (pink and gold, 19 November 2026). If you find gold, blush or beige anywhere, it is the older version: do not use it.

## 1. Brand basics
Name: Kryz Digital Partners (short: Kryz)
Founder: Kristine Adalin
Slogan: One partner to run and grow your business.
Feel: bold, warm and a little electric. Energy: build anyway.

## 2. Logo
The mark is a geometric K. The yellow stem and leg stand for run (steady work). The pink arm ending in an arrow stands for grow. It matches the slogan.
Wordmark: KRYZ in Manrope ExtraBold, letter spacing about 0.035em. "Digital Partners" under or beside it in Instrument Serif italic.

### Logo on dark backgrounds
KRYZ in warm white #FFF9F7 with a stepped neon pink 3D edge and a soft pink glow. "Digital Partners" in bright yellow #FFD23F with a soft yellow glow.

### Logo on light backgrounds
KRYZ in black #141012 with the pink 3D edge, no glow. "Digital Partners" in dark gold #B8860B so it stays readable on white.

### Logo rules
Clear space: at least the width of the arrow head on every side.
Smallest size: mark 24 px tall; full logo 140 px wide. Below 140 px use the flat mark.
Do not stretch, tilt, recolor, swap the pink and yellow, add outlines, or place the logo on busy photos.

Balance: about 70 percent black, 20 percent text, 10 percent pink and yellow. Never pure white or pure black backgrounds.
Signature gradient: yellow to black to pink, for photo backgrounds and special graphics only.

## 4. Fonts (all free on Google Fonts)
Manrope ExtraBold: headlines and the KRYZ wordmark.
Instrument Serif italic: one or two accent words in a headline, in pink.
Inter: body text and buttons.

## 5. Effects
Soft neon pink glow behind key moments and on hover. A glow that follows the mouse on the website. Rounded cards (about 22 px corners), pill shaped buttons. Turn motion off for people who prefer reduced motion.
No emojis in designs. Use real photos or AI photos, never emoji icons.

## 6. Photos and look
Kristine's look: red lips, smokey eyes, oversized black or hot pink blazer over a big shirt.
Headshot: on the yellow to black to pink gradient, hot pink blazer. Arched frame with a yellow outline on the website; circle on profiles.
Mix bold close ups with real life shots at home.

## 7. Voice
First person ("I"), warm and real, honest and friendly, bold when it counts.
Lead with the outcome. Use real proof only. No made up numbers.
No dashes of any kind in copy. No public rates.

## 8. Social sizes
Profile photo 1080 x 1080. Facebook cover 1640 x 624 (keep text in the center). Feed and carousel 1080 x 1350. Reels, Stories and TikTok 1080 x 1920. When cross posting, keep text inside the center safe area so it shows in every feed.
