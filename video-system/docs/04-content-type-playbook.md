# Doc 4: Content Type Editing Playbook

Version 0.1, 27 November 2026. One design system, different frameworks. Pick the framework that fits the message; do not force every video into the same formula.

Pace key: **Fast** = a visual change every 1.5 to 3 s; **Medium** = every 3 to 6 s; **Calm** = every 6 to 12 s. A visual change is a cut, punch in, graphic, B roll or caption emphasis.

Archetype key: **Hero** (transformation, action), **Sage** (clarity, proof), **Outlaw** (challenge the usual way).

## The default sequence (adapt per type)

1. **Before the hook (0 s):** no logo, no intro, no "hi guys". Open on the most interesting frame.
2. **Hook (0 to 3 s):** spoken hook plus hook title on screen (same idea, max 7 words). A punch in or visual change by second 2.
3. **Straight after the hook (3 to 8 s):** the stakes or the promise: why keep watching. First lower third here if it is a first appearance.
4. **Body:** one idea per beat, pattern interrupt every 6 to 10 s on short form.
5. **Payoff:** deliver exactly what the hook promised.
6. **CTA:** one CTA, relevant to the content (approved wording only).

## Detail per type

Each type below lists: hook structure, effects, B roll, graphics, audio, pattern interrupts, platform notes, and type specific QA. The master QA in Doc 8 always applies too.

**1. Talking head.** Hook: a direct statement or question to camera. Effects: dead air cut, punch in on every second cut, hook title. B roll: only when it shows what is being said. Audio: voice only or very low music. Interrupts: punch ins, keyword highlights. QA: eyes on camera in the first frame.

**2. Educational (Sage).** Hook: "Most people [mistake]. Do this instead." Numbered pop ups for each step (title card at the bottom third). Karaoke captions. Screen or B roll for each step. QA: every step is actually explained, not just named.

**3. Personal storytelling (Hero).** Hook: drop into the moment ("The day I..."). Keep natural pauses (`--keep-pause 1.2`). Few graphics. Slight push in on the turning point. Music low and emotional, ducked. QA: did we over cut the emotion?

**4. Personal brand and lifestyle.** Real life shots at home mixed with bold close ups (brand guideline). Captions minimal. Music led. QA: no client or personal information visible in the background.

**5. Behind the scenes.** Hook: the finished thing first, then "here is what it took". Speed ramps on process B roll. QA: no screens with client data.

**6. Thought leadership (Outlaw).** Hook: challenge an outdated practice. Calm confident pace, push in on the main claim, one yellow keyword per line. QA: the claim is backed by a reason or real evidence, not hype.

**7. Product introduction.** Hook: the problem in one line. Logo is not the opener. Product reveal with a smooth push in, three benefit pop ups, demo label if any screen uses sample data. CTA: approved product CTA.

**8. Software and system demo (Kryz Digital Software).** Sequence: problem (talking head, 3 to 5 s) → the system doing the job (screen, fill mode) → zoom and callout on each feature → translate each feature to a business benefit in a caption or pop up → realistic use case → transformation → CTA "Message SYSTEM for a walkthrough". **Demo label on screen the whole time when data is fictional.** QA: every feature shown exists and works as shown; no invented numbers; no real customer data.

**9. Service sales video.** Hook: the pain in the client's words. Show the four pillars (Executive Assistance, Operations, Digital Marketing, AI Systems and Automation) only if relevant. Proof only from the approved list (Business Playbook) and only named with client approval. No public rates (brand rule). QA: offer description matches the current Business Playbook.

**10. Tutorial.** Show the result first. Chapters on YouTube. Screen zoom per step. Recap list at the end. QA: a viewer can follow it with sound off.

**11. Client case study.** Before, process, after. Real results only, written permission on file before editing starts. QA: permission recorded in the tracker.

**12. Testimonial.** Never edit words to change meaning. Lower third with name and role (with permission). QA: permission and the unedited source kept in 10_Editable Project Files.

**13. Podcast clip.** Start on the strongest sentence, then context. Speaker lower thirds. Waveform not needed. QA: the clip makes sense without the full episode.

**14. Long form YouTube.** Cold open (best 10 to 20 s of the video), logo reveal max 2.5 s, chapters every 1 to 3 minutes, B roll and graphics every 20 to 40 s, mid video CTA once, end screen 20 s. QA: chapters in the description match the video.

**15. Affiliate.** Honest pros and cons, real usage only. **Disclosure on screen and in the caption** (for example "Affiliate link: I may earn a commission"). QA: disclosure present on every platform version.

**16. Digital product promo.** Show what is inside (real pages or screens). Content Kit and Strategy Session prices are drafts "to confirm" in the Business Playbook, so no price on screen until confirmed. QA: price and offer name approved.

**17. Faceless.** Text led hook on a branded background or screen footage, karaoke captions or text only, voiceover optional. QA: the first frame reads as Kryz without the logo.

**18. Video ad.** Hook in the first second, captions burned in, product or CTA visible by second 3, CTA graphic for the last 3 s. Make 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1. QA: ad claims are true and approved; platform ad policy checked.
