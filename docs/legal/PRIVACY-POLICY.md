# Kryz Quick Reply Privacy Policy

**DRAFT, version 2026-11-25-draft. Not yet reviewed by a lawyer. Requires product owner approval and legal review before publication.**

Kryz Quick Reply ("the extension") is a Google Chrome extension made by Kryz Digital Partners as part of Kryz Digital Software. This policy explains what the extension accesses, where your information is kept, and the choices you have.

## Your Data. Your Control.

- We do not sell your personal information.
- We do not use your private content to train AI models.
- We do not collect unrelated browsing activity.
- We do not access your passwords or payment credentials.
- Your saved templates and personalizations remain in your browser in the local-only version.
- We do not transmit your saved content to our servers in the local-only version.
- We request only permissions necessary for the features you choose to use.
- You can export or delete your saved information.

## What the extension stores

The extension stores the following in Chrome's local extension storage on your device:

- **Templates** you create or import: title, description, message text, shortcut, folder, tags, favorite and pinned status, and creation and update times.
- **Folders** you create.
- **Recently deleted templates**, kept for 30 days so you can restore them, then erased automatically.
- **Settings**: theme, motion preference, shortcut settings, the list of websites where you switched shortcuts on, your typing-speed assumption, and, if you choose to enter them, your own name and meeting link.
- **Usage statistics**: how many times each template was used, the number of words inserted, daily totals and totals per folder. The text you inserted is not stored as part of statistics.
- **Your acknowledgments**: the version and date when you acknowledged this Privacy Policy and accepted the Terms of Use.
- **Theme mirror**: your theme and motion preference are also kept in the extension's own page storage so the correct theme shows without a flash on startup.

## What the extension does not collect

- It does not read web pages except as described below.
- It does not record keystrokes, browsing history, clipboard history or private conversations.
- It never operates inside password, payment card, one-time-code or similar security fields.
- Values you type into the personalization form (for example a recipient's first name) are used once to build your message and are not saved.

## Where your information goes

In this local-only version, **nothing you save is sent to Kryz Digital Partners or anyone else**. The extension makes no network requests of its own: its pages are restricted from connecting to any server, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Your information leaves the extension only when you choose to: when you copy a message to your clipboard, insert it into a website, or export a file. What happens to a message after you insert it into a website is governed by that website.

## How website access works

- **Insert from the toolbar popup.** When you click the toolbar icon and choose Insert, Chrome grants temporary access to the current tab (the `activeTab` permission). The extension places your message into the field you had selected. It does not read the page's content.
- **Text shortcuts (optional).** If you switch shortcuts on for a specific website, Chrome asks you to grant access to that website. On that site only, the extension checks the few characters just before your cursor in normal text fields to recognize a shortcut such as `/intro`. It does not store or send what you type. Only the matched shortcut name is passed inside the extension to retrieve that template. You can revoke access at any time in Settings or the Privacy & Security Center, or in Chrome's extension settings.

## Security

Chrome's local extension storage is protected by your operating system account and Chrome profile, but **the extension does not add its own encryption**. Please do not store passwords, private keys, API keys or other highly sensitive information in templates. We do not claim the extension is completely secure.

## Your choices and rights

From the Privacy & Security Center you can export all of your data, delete individual templates, clear usage statistics, empty recently deleted items, revoke website access, delete all extension data, or reset the extension. Deleting data in the extension does not delete files you exported earlier. When you uninstall the extension, Chrome removes the data it stored on your device.

Because the local-only version does not send your information to us, we do not hold a copy of it and cannot retrieve, correct or delete it on your behalf; you control it directly in the extension.
