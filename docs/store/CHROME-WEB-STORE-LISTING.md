## Description
Write it once. Reply in seconds. Stay in control.

Kryz Quick Reply helps virtual assistants, freelancers, recruiters, sales and support teams stop retyping the same messages. Save your intros, follow-ups, invoice reminders, interview invites and support replies, then personalize and insert them wherever you work.

• Templates with folders, tags, favorites and pins
• Variables like {{first_name}} and {{company}} with a preview before you insert
• Copy in one click, or insert into the text box you're typing in
• Optional text shortcuts such as /intro and /followup on websites you choose, nothing expands until you press Tab
• 13 optional starter templates for LinkedIn, recruitment, sales, onboarding, invoices and support
• See your momentum: uses, weekly activity and estimated time saved, counted on your device
• Light and Kryz Signature (dark) themes

Your Data. Your Control.
• Templates stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
• No account, no ads, no trackers, no external analytics, no AI processing.
• Never works in password, payment or security-code fields.
• Website access is requested one site at a time, only if you turn shortcuts on, and you can revoke it anytime.
• Export or delete everything from the Privacy & Security Center.

Built for productivity. Designed for privacy. A Kryz Digital Software product by Kryz Digital Partners.
